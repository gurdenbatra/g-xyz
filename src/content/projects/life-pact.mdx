Life Pact exists to solve a specific, unglamorous problem: nature-based solutions work, but they rarely travel. A raingarden that transforms one neighbourhood doesn't automatically teach the next city anything, unless someone does the work of writing down what made it work.

## The problem

Cities are full of good pilots and short on replication. Life Pact, an EU LIFE-funded initiative, set out to identify, codify, and package the conditions that let a nature-based solution spread from the place it was invented to a place it wasn't — with Leuven as originator and Madrid and Krakow as replication sites.

## What I built

As developer and designer, I built the site carrying this work: a replication toolkit including a step-by-step guide for municipal teams, City Replication Plans, a practices guide organizing NbS work into six categories, and deep-dive case studies — structured so a planning team in an unfamiliar city can find the specific lesson it needs rather than read a full report.

## Partners

Built in collaboration with the City of Leuven and Democratic Society, alongside Alessandra Puricelli and Alicia Carvajal Rowan.
